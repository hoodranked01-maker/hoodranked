PROCESSING TIMES

Orders are processed within 7–14 business days of payment confirmation. During major drops, processing can take slightly longer — we'll flag this on the track order page if it applies.

DELIVERY ESTIMATES

WITHIN NIGERIA

7–14 DAYS

Lagos deliveries are usually fastest; other states may take a little longer.

INTERNATIONAL

2–3 WEEKS

Customs processing in your country can occasionally add extra time.

SHIPPING COSTS

Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on your location and order weight. Any promotional free-shipping thresholds will be shown in your cart before you pay.

ORDER TRACKING

You'll get a tracking link by email once your order ships. If it hasn't arrived after the estimate above, reach out and we'll chase it with the courier on your behalf.

RETURNS & EXCHANGES

Not feeling the fit? You can request a return or exchange within 7 days of delivery, as long as the item is:

  • — Unworn, unwashed, and in its original condition
  • — Still carrying its original tags
  • — Accompanied by your order number or receipt

Limited edition and final sale items are not eligible for return unless they arrive faulty or damaged — this will be clearly marked on the product page.

HOW TO START A RETURN

Email hoodranked2@gmail.com with your order number and the reason for the return, and we'll send you instructions on where to send it back.

REFUNDS

Once we receive and inspect your return, we'll process your refund to your original payment method within 5–10 business days. Original shipping costs aren't refundable unless the item arrived faulty.

DAMAGED OR INCORRECT ITEMS

If something arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, email us with a photo within 48 hours of delivery and we'll sort a replacement or full refund at no extra cost to you.

CONTACT US

Any other questions about your order? Email hoodranked2@gmail.com or visit our Contact page.
